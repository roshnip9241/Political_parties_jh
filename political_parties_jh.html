Welcome to the Political World of Jharkhand

15 November 2000
The Day Jharkhand Became a Separate State
Jharkhand is a state in eastern India known for its rich tribal heritage, forests, minerals and diverse culture. Politics plays an important role in the development and administration of the state.

Jharkhand was formed on 15 November 2000 after being separated from Bihar. Since its formation, several regional and national political parties have participated in the political life of the state.

This project presents basic information about some important political parties, leaders and political issues associated with Jharkhand.

Political Parties of Jharkhand

Eight Important Political Parties



BJP 		01. Bharatiya Janata Party

Abbreviation: BJP

Category: National Political Party

The Bharatiya Janata Party is one of India's major national political parties and has been an important political force in Jharkhand.

Important Jharkhand Leader: Babulal Marandi



JMM 		02. Jharkhand Mukti Morcha

Abbreviation: JMM

Category: Regional Political Party

JMM is one of the prominent regional political parties of Jharkhand. The party has historical roots in the movement for a separate Jharkhand state.

Important Leaders: Shibu Soren and Hemant Soren



TMC 		03. All India Trinamool Congress

Abbreviation: TMC

Category: National Political Party

The All India Trinamool Congress is a national political party with its main political base in West Bengal. It has also participated in political activities and elections outside West Bengal.

Important Leader: Mamata Banerjee



INC 		04. Indian National Congress

Abbreviation: INC

Category: National Political Party

The Indian National Congress is one of India's oldest political parties. It has participated in Jharkhand politics and elections since the formation of the state.



AJSU 		05. All Jharkhand Students Union Party

Abbreviation: AJSU

Category: Regional Political Party

AJSU has its roots in the student movement in the Jharkhand region. It later developed into a political organisation and has participated in Jharkhand elections.

Important Leader: Sudesh Mahto



RJD 		06. Rashtriya Janata Dal

Abbreviation: RJD

Category: Political Party

The Rashtriya Janata Dal has its roots in Bihar and has also maintained a political presence in Jharkhand. It has contested elections and participated in political alliances.



CPI(ML)L 		07. CPI (ML) Liberation

Full Name: Communist Party of India (Marxist-Leninist) Liberation

Abbreviation: CPI(ML)L

Category: Left-wing Political Party

CPI(ML) Liberation is a left-wing political party that has political activity in several parts of India, including parts of Jharkhand.



JD(U) 		08. Janata Dal (United)

Abbreviation: JD(U)

Category: Political Party

Janata Dal (United) is a political party that has an organisational and electoral presence in Jharkhand.
